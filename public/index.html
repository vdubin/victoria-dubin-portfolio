01 A little about me

Connecting rehabilitation
and primary care.

I’m a PT, DPT and doctoral student in Rehabilitation Sciences at Mass General Brigham University. Before doctoral study, I worked across traditional and mobile outpatient care, telehealth, and urgent care and primary care settings.

That clinical perspective shapes my interest in proactive, longitudinal mobility surveillance and the role of physical therapy in primary care. Outside of work, I love being outdoors.
